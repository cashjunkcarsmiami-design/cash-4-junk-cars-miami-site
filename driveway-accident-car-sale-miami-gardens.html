Selling an Accident-Damaged Car From a Miami Gardens Driveway

A car moved home after a collision may still have loading hazards. Describe visible damage and any insurance or ownership restrictions before accepting an offer.

Confirm the vehicle can be sold

Make sure any insurer, lender, storage provider or other interested party has completed its process. Do not sell a vehicle subject to an unresolved claim, lien or ownership dispute.

Photograph loading-related damage

Show bent wheels, deployed airbags, broken suspension, leaking areas and missing parts from a safe position. Never crawl under the car or touch damaged high-voltage components.

Prepare the driveway

Move other vehicles, open gates and identify low branches or narrow turns. Keep people away during loading and confirm final terms before releasing the car.
