Keeping a Trailer Hitch Before Selling a Car in Cutler Bay

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 10, 2026

A hitch receiver, ball mount or attached carrier can have a different role in a car sale than ordinary belongings in the trunk. If you want to retain hitch equipment from your Cutler Bay vehicle, explain that plan while requesting the offer rather than changing the setup on pickup day.

Separate removable items from installed equipment

Describe a removable ball mount or carrier separately from the receiver attached to the vehicle. Say which items stay and which you want to keep. Do not assume every hitch component is personal property outside the sale, or that a photographed accessory must remain. The buyer should review the configuration you actually intend to sell.

Report the result of any agreed removal

If equipment is removed, update photographs and mention visible damage, missing fittings or wiring changes. Do not undertake under-vehicle removal work simply because you want the item retained. Discuss the planned condition and ask whether the offer remains applicable. A sale inquiry is not a reason to attempt a difficult dismantling task without appropriate help.

Confirm what will be present at collection

Review components, access and vehicle movement before scheduling. Tell the buyer if a carrier is still attached or a trailer is parked nearby, so neither is mistaken for part of the vehicle purchase. Keep the agreed price connected to the confirmed car configuration. If your plan changes, notify the buyer before the driver is sent.

Does a whole-car offer automatically include my hitch accessories?

Ask rather than assume. Describe each item you want included or retained and confirm the final vehicle setup with the buyer before accepting the amount and arranging collection.
