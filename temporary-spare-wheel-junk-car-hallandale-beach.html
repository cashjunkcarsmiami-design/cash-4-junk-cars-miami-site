A Temporary Spare Wheel on a Hallandale Beach Junk Car

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 10, 2026

A temporary spare fitted to an unwanted car can be easy to overlook in a quote conversation. Before a Hallandale Beach sale, explain which wheels are installed and where the original wheel is. Vehicle evaluation and pickup should be based on that current setup.

Identify the fitted spare and original wheel

Tell the buyer which position has a temporary spare and whether the original wheel is available, damaged or missing. Include clear recent photographs. Do not describe all wheels as normal because the vehicle has four fitted wheels. Explain whether a loose wheel is intended to be included and ask how the buyer wants that detail documented.

Describe movement without assuming roadworthiness

Say whether the car has been moved recently or whether rolling has not been checked. A temporary spare does not establish that the vehicle can be driven to meet a buyer. Do not take it on a road merely to simplify collection. Give the buyer the actual condition so pickup can be planned without relying on an unverified driving assumption.

Confirm the complete sale configuration

Before scheduling, review any other missing components and the location where the car will be collected. Ask whether pickup can be included with the installed spare and any loose wheel disclosed. Update the buyer if another wheel is removed or the car's movement changes. Keep your confirmed amount connected to the vehicle configuration available at handoff.

Does a fitted spare make the car count as complete?

Explain the fitted spare and the original wheel situation rather than applying that label yourself. The buyer can review completeness and collection needs from the current components and condition.
