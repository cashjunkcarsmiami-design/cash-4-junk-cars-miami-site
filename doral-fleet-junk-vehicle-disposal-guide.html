Preparing a Fleet or Company Vehicle for Pickup in Doral

Business-owned vehicles require a clear authorized contact and complete ownership information.

CallDoral Service

Identify the Authorized Seller

Confirm who has authority to approve the sale and sign vehicle documents. The business name on the ownership record should be reviewed before scheduling.

Separate Company Property

Remove tools, toll devices, fuel cards, decals, registration copies, customer materials and electronic equipment. Follow company procedures for plates and branding.

Document Each Vehicle

For multiple units, create a list containing VIN, year, make, model, condition, keys and exact parking position. Send separate photos so the vehicles are not confused.

Plan Commercial Access

Provide loading hours, security instructions, dock or yard restrictions and the on-site contact. Explain whether a tractor, trailer or parked inventory limits turning space.

Company Vehicle Information to Prepare

Businesses should identify the vehicle year, make, model, VIN, mileage and operating condition. Explain whether it is owned, financed, leased or included on a fleet account, and confirm who has authority to approve the sale. Disclose missing parts, collision damage, mechanical problems and whether company graphics or equipment must be removed before pickup.

Commercial Pickup Coordination

Share loading-zone rules, security procedures, dock restrictions and the name of the onsite contact. Keep the title and business authorization documents available, remove inventory and personal information and confirm how payment should be documented. Doral Fleet Questions

Can several vehicles be reviewed together?

Yes, but each vehicle should have its own photos, ownership details and condition description.

Who should meet the driver?

An authorized representative familiar with the vehicles and property rules should be available.
