Selling a Junk Car From a Tow or Storage Yard

Release rules, accumulating charges and limited hours make early coordination important.

CallLocal Service

Request the Current Release Information

Contact the facility directly for its balance, acceptable payment methods, release requirements and operating hours. Ask whether charges continue to accumulate and when the quoted balance expires.

Confirm Ownership and Authorization

Determine who must appear or sign and which identification and ownership documents the yard requires. A buyer cannot override a facility's release rules.

Describe the Vehicle

Explain accident damage, missing parts, wheel condition and whether keys are held by the facility. Ask the yard where the vehicle is positioned and whether another truck can access it.

Coordinate Both Parties

Do not assume the buyer and facility have spoken. Provide the correct contact, agreed pickup window and release status, while keeping private account information protected.

Confirm Fees and Release Requirements

Ask the tow or storage yard for the current balance, accepted payment method, release hours and the documents required from the registered owner. Determine whether fees continue increasing and whether an authorized buyer or towing company may collect the vehicle. Resolve these questions directly with the facility before scheduling another truck.

Give the Buyer Complete Vehicle Details

Provide the year, make, model, title status, damage, missing parts and yard contact information. Confirm whether the car rolls and when it can be accessed.
