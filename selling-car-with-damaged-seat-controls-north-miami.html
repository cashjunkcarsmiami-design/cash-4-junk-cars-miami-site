Selling a Car With Stuck Power Seats in North Miami

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 5, 2026

A power seat that will not move can affect access and leave belongings out of reach. For a North Miami vehicle offer, explain the seat position, the known fault and any other interior or electrical symptoms.

Describe the position and symptoms

Say which seat is affected and whether it is stuck forward, backward or reclined. Mention when the controls last worked and any existing diagnosis. A silent motor does not establish the exact failed component. Keep the seat issue separate from starting condition, other electrical faults and vehicle movement so the buyer receives a complete description rather than a single symptom.

Review accessible belongings

Look through areas you can reach and explain if something remains beneath or behind a stuck seat. Do not promise an empty cabin if parts of it have not been checked. Send current overall interior photos and a clear view of the affected seat without dismantling it for the quote. Ask what information is needed if the position makes an area difficult to inspect.

Coordinate a realistic handoff

Give the year, model, ownership document availability and pickup location. Explain whether doors open, keys are present and the car can roll. Ask for an as-is review and confirm collection requirements. If someone changes the seat position or completes a repair after the quote, report the new condition before treating the earlier description as final.

Do I have to repair the seat before requesting an offer?

You can request an evaluation in the current condition. Explain the access limitations and confirm any remaining inspection questions with the buyer.
