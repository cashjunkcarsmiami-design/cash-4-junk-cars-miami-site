Selling a Car With a Hood That Will Not Open in Hialeah

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 5, 2026

A stuck hood leaves part of a car uninspected. When requesting a Hialeah vehicle offer, explain the access problem and the condition you know rather than making assumptions about components you cannot see.

Disclose the inspection limit

Tell the buyer when the hood last opened and whether the latch, cable or body was damaged, if known. Explain the last known running condition separately. If a previous repair report exists, state its date; it does not establish the condition of an area that has not been checked recently. Avoid describing the engine compartment as complete if you cannot confirm that information.

Use accessible views

Provide overall exterior photos, visible front-end damage and the current parking location. Existing photographs taken before the hood became stuck can help explain history if labeled with their age. Do not force the hood open solely to produce a quote photograph. Ask what other details the buyer can use for the initial review and explain any questions that remain unanswered.

Confirm collection and evaluation terms

Give the year, model, document availability, keys and whether the car can roll. Ask whether the access problem can be considered for an as-is offer and what remains to be checked. Confirm the pickup address and included collection before scheduling. If the hood is opened or additional damage is discovered later, update the buyer before relying on the earlier description.

Can I ask for an offer without opening the hood?

You can ask for an initial review. Disclose the access limitation and ask what additional inspection or information is needed to confirm an offer.
