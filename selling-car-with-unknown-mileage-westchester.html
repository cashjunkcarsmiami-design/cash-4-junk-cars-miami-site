Explaining Unknown Mileage in a Westchester Car Sale

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 5, 2026

An unreadable or uncertain mileage figure should be labeled clearly. When requesting a Westchester vehicle offer, give what you know without converting an old service record or a family memory into a current odometer reading.

Separate readings from estimates

Explain whether the dashboard is unreadable, the car is inaccessible or the mileage simply has not been checked. If you have an earlier dated reading, identify its source and date. A remembered number is an estimate, not proof of current mileage. Keep that distinction in each quote conversation so different buyers are not evaluating different unsupported figures.

Provide other condition information

Give the year, model, known operating symptoms, visible damage and missing parts. State when the vehicle was last used if known. Existing maintenance paperwork may help describe history but does not resolve an unknown current reading. Share accessible photographs and ask which additional information the buyer needs instead of inventing a figure to complete the inquiry.

Confirm the offer with uncertainty disclosed

Repeat that mileage is unknown when discussing a final amount and collection. Ask whether anything remains to be inspected and which documents are required. Provide keys, wheel movement and pickup-access details. If an actual reading becomes available before handoff, update the buyer and confirm that the offer reflects the new information before proceeding.

Can I use an old oil-change reading as today's mileage?

Identify it as a dated historical reading. Do not present it as current mileage unless that has been confirmed.
