Describing a Bent Wheel Before Selling a Car in Cutler Bay

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 5, 2026

A damaged wheel can change vehicle movement and collection requirements. When requesting a Cutler Bay car offer, explain the wheel position, other known damage and whether the vehicle has been assessed since the problem occurred.

Describe what is visible and confirmed

Identify which wheel or rim is affected and whether a tire or other component is missing. Mention an existing repair diagnosis if available, but do not assume that visible rim damage is the only fault. Explain how the problem arose when known and when the car last moved. Separate observations from unconfirmed suspension or steering explanations.

Show the current parking position

Send an overall vehicle photo and an accessible view of the affected corner, along with the pickup location. Mention other parked cars, limited driveway space or a gate. Do not move or operate the vehicle just to simplify a quote photograph. Existing images and a clear description can support an initial review; label any photo that predates the damage.

Confirm pickup based on present condition

Give the year, model, keys and ownership document availability. Ask whether the offer includes collection for the wheel and movement condition described, and clarify what still needs review. If a wheel is replaced or more parts are removed before handoff, update the buyer. The agreed offer should describe the same vehicle that will be present at the appointment.

Do I have to buy a replacement wheel before requesting an offer?

You can request an as-is review first. Explain the damage and ask whether the vehicle can be evaluated and collected in its current condition.
