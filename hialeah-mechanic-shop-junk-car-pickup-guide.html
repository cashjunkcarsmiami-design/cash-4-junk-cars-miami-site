Picking Up a Junk Car From a Hialeah Mechanic Shop

Shop authorization and clear release instructions help prevent a tow truck from arriving before the vehicle is ready.

CallHialeah Service

Speak With the Shop First

Confirm that the facility is prepared to release the vehicle and ask whether the owner must be present. Obtain the correct shop contact and pickup hours. Do not schedule until any repair, diagnostic, storage or authorization questions have been resolved directly with the facility.

Explain the Vehicle Condition

A partially disassembled vehicle may need special planning. State whether wheels, keys, battery, engine components or body panels have been removed and whether the car rolls and steers.

Share Loading Instructions

Ask where the tow truck should enter, where the vehicle is parked and whether other cars must be moved. Busy repair lots may provide a narrow pickup window.

Keep the Transaction Clear

Confirm the quoted amount, towing terms, ownership documents and who will sign the release. Keep the shop and buyer informed if the pickup time changes.

Hialeah Shop Pickup Questions

Can a shop release the car without me?

That depends on the facility and ownership documents. Confirm its policy before pickup.

What if parts were removed?

List every known missing part so the offer and towing plan can be reviewed accurately.
