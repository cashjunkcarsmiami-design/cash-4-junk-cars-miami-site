Coordinating Junk Car Pickup at a Doral Commercial Property

Office parks, warehouses and leased lots often have vendor rules that must be handled before a truck arrives.

Confirm property permission

Ask management or security whether a towing vendor needs advance notice, insurance information, identification or a scheduled access window. Obtain permission for the exact vehicle and parking space.

Share the correct entrance

Provide the business name, suite, gate, loading area and on-site contact. Mention height limits, dock traffic, narrow service roads and times when deliveries block access.

Separate company and vehicle authorization

Confirm who owns the vehicle and who can authorize its sale. Business property approval does not replace ownership documents. Keep the loading area clear and follow workplace safety procedures.
