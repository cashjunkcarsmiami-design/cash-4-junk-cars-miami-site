A Long-Parked Car Surrounded by Tall Grass in Homestead

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 10, 2026

Tall grass can hide the lower part of a long-parked Homestead car. For a junk car inquiry, explain what is visible and what you cannot safely check. The buyer needs the vehicle's position and access conditions rather than an assumption that an ordinary pickup will work.

Take photographs from accessible positions

Send an overall view and a view of the approach from the property entrance. If vegetation hides tires or the surface beneath the car, say so directly. Do not step into an area you cannot safely see just to obtain a close-up. Ask the buyer what additional information is needed to review collection from the current position.

Mark movement and surface details as known or unknown

Explain whether the car has recently rolled or whether it has stayed in place for months. Say whether the ground appears paved, firm or uncertain without guessing conditions beneath the vehicle. Mention missing wheels or visible damage you can actually observe. Unknown details should remain questions for review rather than confident statements based on older memories.

Coordinate property preparation before scheduling

If the access route needs vegetation cleared, arrange that with the person responsible for the property and discuss the final position with the buyer. Do not move a stuck or unstable car as a substitute for access planning. Confirm whether pickup can be included after the relevant details are reviewed, and update photographs if the route or car position changes.

Does tall grass mean the car cannot be collected?

Not automatically. It means some condition and access details may need review. Provide current views, explain what is hidden and let the buyer confirm whether collection can be arranged from that location.
