How Long Is a Junk Car Offer Valid in Miami?

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · September 21, 2026

A number discussed last month is not necessarily an offer you can accept today. When selling an unwanted car in Miami, ask how long the quote remains valid and what information it assumes. A short confirmation can prevent confusion between an old estimate and a current agreement.

Ask for a time frame

When you receive an offer, ask whether it is valid until a specific date or needs confirmation when you are ready to sell. Do not assume every buyer uses the same policy. Keep the vehicle description with the quoted amount so you can remember which condition, location and included equipment were discussed.

Report changes before accepting

Tell the buyer if the car stopped running, suffered additional damage, lost components or moved to another location after the first conversation. A quote for a complete car at an accessible address may need a fresh review if those details change. Explain changes before the appointment, not when the driver is already loading.

Separate an estimate from a confirmed purchase

An advertised range is a starting point for a discussion, not a personalized offer for every vehicle. Ask which details still need verification, whether pickup is included in the agreed purchase and when the amount will be confirmed. Do not plan around the highest advertised figure without a vehicle-specific review.

Make the final message easy to follow

Reference the year, make, model and the earlier quote date. Confirm that the car remains in the same condition and state your current availability. If something changed, describe it directly.
