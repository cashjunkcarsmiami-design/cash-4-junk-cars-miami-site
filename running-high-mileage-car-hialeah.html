Selling a Running, High-Mileage Car in Hialeah

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · September 21, 2026

A car does not have to be completely inoperative for its owner to want to sell it. If your high-mileage vehicle still runs in Hialeah, describe that accurately. The useful question is how its overall condition fits your available sale options, not whether you can label it a junk car.

Describe running and driving separately

Explain whether the engine starts reliably and whether the car drives normally. Mention intermittent faults you already know about, even when they were not present on the last trip. If it has been parked since a problem appeared, state the last known condition rather than testing it on the road for a quote.

Give mileage with the right level of certainty

Read the odometer if you can do so normally. If the display does not work or you only remember an older reading, explain that limitation. Do not round a number down to make the car sound newer. Mileage is one detail among the model, damage, mechanical condition and completeness.

Compare appropriate buyers

A functioning vehicle may also have a private-sale or trade-in option. Consider the time, preparation and arrangements each option would require. Ask for an as-is purchase review if that is the route you prefer, but do not assume the first junk car offer is the vehicle's only possible market.

Keep the description consistent

Use the same known facts when discussing offers. State whether accessories or vehicle components will remain and confirm pickup terms before accepting.
