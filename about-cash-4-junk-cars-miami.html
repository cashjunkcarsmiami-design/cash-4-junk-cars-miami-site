About our Miami vehicle-buying service

Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami buys whole used, damaged and non-running vehicles and arranges pickup at the seller’s location. We do not sell individual auto parts or provide standalone towing.

Where we collect vehicles

Our base is 2965 NW 101 St, Miami, FL 33147. We collect vehicles within approximately 20 miles of that address. We do not receive customers there. Call with the vehicle location to confirm coverage; do not bring a vehicle to the base.

What to confirm before selling

Provide the year, make, model, current condition, title status and pickup location. Confirm the amount offered, towing inclusion, ownership requirements and pickup time before accepting. Vehicle offers depend on the vehicle and the information provided; same-day availability is confirmed individually.
