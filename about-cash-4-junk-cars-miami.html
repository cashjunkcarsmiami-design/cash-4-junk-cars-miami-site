Quick answers about our mobile junk car buying service

Who buys whole junk cars near Miami?

Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami evaluates whole used, damaged and non-running vehicles for purchase. Call 305-457-0817 with the year, make, model, current condition and pickup location for a vehicle-specific offer.

Where can you collect my vehicle?

Our usual mobile pickup area is approximately 20 miles from our Miami base at 2965 NW 101 St, Miami, FL 33147. Give us the exact vehicle location so we can confirm coverage and availability; a city page alone does not confirm a pickup.

Can I bring my car to your address or buy parts there?

No. We do not receive customers at the base address or sell individual auto parts. Collection is arranged at the seller’s location. We do not provide standalone towing.

What should I confirm about payment and towing?

Confirm the full purchase amount, when payment happens, whether pickup is included for your vehicle, ownership requirements and the collection window before accepting. Report missing parts and changes to the vehicle condition before pickup.

Can I ask about selling a car for recycling?

Yes. Tell us that you want a whole-vehicle offer and ask how the vehicle will be handled. Purchase value and the next use of a vehicle depend on its condition and reusable components. Ask questions before accepting rather than assuming that every purchased car goes directly to scrap processing.

Can I discuss my vehicle in Spanish?
