Preparing a Junk Car With Flat Tires for Pickup in Homestead

One flat tire may be routine; several damaged, locked or missing wheels can change the loading plan. Accurate photos help dispatch understand the difference.

Count affected wheels

Report which tires are flat, whether rims are damaged and whether any wheel is missing. Explain if brakes are seized, steering is locked or the vehicle has settled into dirt.

Describe the ground and route

Tell dispatch if the car is on pavement, gravel, grass or soft soil. Include gate width, slope, drainage ditches and the distance to a stable loading area.

Avoid unsafe temporary repairs

Do not crawl under an unstable vehicle or improvise wheel supports. Clear personal items and obstacles, then allow the pickup professional to decide how the vehicle should be moved.
