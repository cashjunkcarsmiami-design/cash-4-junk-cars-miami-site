Junk Car Pickup from a Private Garage in Coral Gables

A car does not have to be on the street to be considered for pickup. An unwanted vehicle may be inside a private garage, behind a gate, under a carport or on a narrow driveway. Those locations can work, but the pickup is easier to evaluate when the seller explains the access before a tow vehicle is dispatched.

Describe the Garage and Driveway Access

When requesting an offer, mention whether the car is inside or outside, whether a gate must be opened and whether the driveway has room for a tow vehicle to approach. If the garage has a low opening or sharp turn, share that information as well.

Explain whether another vehicle blocks the car, the steering is unlocked, all four tires are present, the car can be placed in neutral and the keys are available. Also mention branches, gates or parked cars that limit access. Photos of the vehicle and its path to the street can help prevent a failed pickup.

Gather Ownership Information Before Scheduling

Explain the title status and ask what identification or ownership documents may be required. Requirements can depend on the vehicle and the seller’s situation, so it is better to ask before the appointment than to rely on a general answer.

Review the guide to documents to gather before pickup. Do not sign or alter paperwork until the buyer explains what is needed for the transaction.

Make the Vehicle Reachable

If it is safe and permitted, move other household vehicles before the scheduled arrival. Unlock gates, remove loose objects around the car and make sure the driver can reach it without crossing a locked area. If the car cannot be moved, say so when arranging pickup rather than trying to force it out.

Never work beneath an unstable vehicle or attempt a repair simply to prepare it for sale. The useful information is whether it starts, rolls, steers and brakes.

Confirm the Offer and Pickup Details

Confirm the vehicle description, pickup address, contact person, access instructions and the documents the buyer expects to review. Ask whether towing is included for the specific pickup and whether the quoted amount depends on information that still needs verification.
