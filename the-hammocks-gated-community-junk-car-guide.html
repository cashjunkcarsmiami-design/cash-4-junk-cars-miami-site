Junk Car Pickup From a Gated Community in The Hammocks

Private roads, association rules, assigned spaces and security gates can affect vehicle removal in The Hammocks. Confirming access before dispatch helps avoid a truck arriving without permission or enough room to load the car.

Contact Management or Security First

Ask whether commercial tow trucks may enter, which entrance they must use and whether vendors are limited to certain hours. Confirm if a resident must meet the driver or if written authorization is required. Share gate instructions privately by phone or WhatsApp; never publish access codes.

Provide the community name, nearest cross street and parking-space location. Areas near Kendall Drive, Hammocks Boulevard and Southwest 137th or 152nd avenues may have similar entrances, so specific directions matter.

Show the Loading Space

Send wide photos of the internal street, parking row and route to the vehicle. Identify speed bumps, low branches, tight turns, covered parking, narrow gates and cars that may block a flatbed. If the vehicle is in a garage, provide the posted height clearance and photos of ramps.

Document Severe Damage Safely

Photograph the front, rear, both sides and every wheel. Mention steering or suspension damage, flat tires, broken glass, leaking fluids, locked wheels and loose panels. Do not crawl under a damaged vehicle or attempt to straighten parts.

Confirm Ownership and Pickup Terms

Gather the title or available registration and identification. The seller must be authorized to transfer the car. Review the offer, towing terms, pickup time and person releasing the vehicle before dispatch.
