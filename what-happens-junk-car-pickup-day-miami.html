What Happens on Junk Car Pickup Day in Miami

A clear pickup-day plan helps the seller, driver and property owner avoid last-minute confusion. Confirm the offer terms, access and paperwork before the truck is dispatched.

Before the driver arrives

Remove personal property, toll devices and anything you intend to keep. Place available ownership documents and identification together. Confirm the pickup address, vehicle description, agreed terms and who is authorized to complete the transaction.

During the vehicle review

The buyer may verify the VIN, condition, missing parts and whether the vehicle matches the information used for the offer. Disclose changes before loading. Review the final payment and paperwork instead of relying on an earlier verbal summary.

Before the vehicle leaves

Receive the agreed documentation and payment before releasing the vehicle. Follow current Florida requirements for plates and ownership records, and ask the appropriate agency when unsure. Keep copies of completed documents and the buyer’s contact information.
