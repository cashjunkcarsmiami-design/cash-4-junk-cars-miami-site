How to Prepare for a Junk Car Pickup in Coral Gables

A successful pickup starts before the flatbed arrives. Coral Gables homes, condominiums, garages and repair shops can each present different access conditions, so clear information helps the buyer review the vehicle and plan the right equipment.

Start With Complete Vehicle Details

Provide the year, make, model, mileage, title status and exact condition. Say whether the engine starts, the transmission shifts, the steering unlocks and all four wheels roll. Mention missing keys, flat tires, loose panels, fire or flood exposure and removed parts. These details help prevent a quote or equipment change at pickup.

Take wide photos of the front, rear and both sides. Add close views of collision damage and a photo showing the VIN plate or registration information when requested privately. Never place personal documents or identification in a public review.

Plan Access at Homes and Condos

A driveway near Granada Boulevard may allow a straightforward approach, while a garage close to US-1 may have height limits, ramps or tight turns. Tell the dispatcher about gates, narrow entrances, low branches, parked vehicles and any surface that may not support a truck. For a condominium, confirm management approval and vendor hours before choosing a time.

If the car cannot safely reach the street, do not try to move it. Send photos from the vehicle to the loading area so the driver can evaluate the route. Keep children, pets and bystanders away from the work zone.

Prepare Ownership Documents and the Vehicle

The seller must be legally authorized to transfer the vehicle. Gather the available title or registration and identification before scheduling. If the title is lost, call first so the individual situation can be reviewed. Remove personal belongings and toll devices, and ask what should happen with the license plate.

Confirm the Offer and Pickup Instructions

Before dispatch, confirm the vehicle being sold, agreed offer, towing terms, address and authorized contact. Towing may be included for qualifying pickups once condition and access are confirmed. Same-day service depends on location and availability.
