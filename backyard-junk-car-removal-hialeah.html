Planning Junk Car Removal From a Hialeah Backyard

A vehicle behind a house may require more planning than a driveway pickup. Dispatch needs an honest description of the route from the car to the street.

Measure and describe the access

Check gate width, overhead branches, fences, utility lines and tight turns. Explain whether the route crosses grass, gravel, pavers or soft ground. Do not remove a fence or enter a neighbor’s property without permission.

Describe the vehicle position

State whether the wheels roll, steering unlocks, tires hold air and keys are available. Mention if the vehicle is sunk into soil, surrounded by stored items or positioned nose-first against a wall.

Keep people away from loading

Clear children, pets and loose objects from the route. Let the towing professional determine safe attachment and movement. A photo from several angles can help evaluate whether normal pickup equipment can reach the vehicle.
