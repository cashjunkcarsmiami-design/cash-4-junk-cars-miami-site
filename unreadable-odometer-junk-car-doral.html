Requesting a Junk Car Offer With an Unreadable Odometer in Doral

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · September 21, 2026

If the dashboard will not light up, you may be unable to provide the current mileage during your first call. For a junk car quote in Doral, state that limitation directly. Guessing a precise reading can create an avoidable disagreement later, even if you were only trying to complete a form.

Label older readings clearly

A service record or older photo may show mileage from a past date. Explain where the number came from and when it was recorded. Do not present that number as a current reading if the car was driven afterward. If you have no reliable reference, say that the mileage is unknown.

Describe the display problem separately

Explain whether the entire dashboard is dark, only the odometer is unreadable or the vehicle currently has no power. Do not assume a battery replacement will solve it or diagnose a failed component without evidence. Also describe the last known engine and driving condition, which may be a separate issue.

Provide the other useful vehicle details

Give the year, make, model, visible damage, missing components and Doral location. Existing photos can support the initial review without requiring you to alter the dashboard. Do not tamper with the odometer or start an unsafe vehicle merely to produce a number for the buyer.

Clarify what remains to be verified

Ask how unknown mileage affects an initial estimate and what information the buyer will need before completing a purchase. Keep any discussion of a historical reading consistent with the records you actually have. The quote conversation is not a substitute for reviewing the information required on the final sale documents.
