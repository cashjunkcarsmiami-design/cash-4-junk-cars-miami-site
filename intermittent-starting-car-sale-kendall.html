Selling a Car That Only Starts Sometimes in Kendall

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · September 28, 2026

A vehicle that started yesterday may fail to start today. When selling an unwanted car in Kendall, intermittent behavior deserves its own description. Calling it simply running or non-running can leave out the fact most relevant to the buyer and pickup team.

Describe the pattern you observed

Explain when the problem began, how often it happens and the last successful start you remember. Mention a shop diagnosis only if one exists. Say whether the vehicle has been driven since the issue started or just started while parked. Avoid promising that the problem is only a battery or a starter when you do not know.

Request a quote for the uncertain condition

Tell the buyer that starting on pickup day cannot be guaranteed. That lets the offer and collection plan account for the uncertainty. You can provide current photos and existing service information without repeatedly trying to start the car. A short successful test should not erase the history of a recurring fault from the description.

Update the team before the appointment

If the car stops starting completely or its parking position changes, send an update. Keep the keys available and describe access even if you expect the car to move under its own power. Discuss collection with the vehicle purchase rather than booking a separate tow. Confirm the offer terms before accepting so a temporary improvement does not create different expectations.

En español

Si tu carro en Kendall prende a veces y otras no, dilo así. La recogida debe planearse sin garantizar que encenderá ese día.

Should I wait until it starts before calling?

No. Explain the intermittent fault when you call. The buyer can review the current situation without a promise that the car will start at pickup.
