Getting Condo Approval for Junk Car Pickup in Miami Beach

A vehicle owner may be ready to sell while the condominium still requires vendor approval. Handle property rules before scheduling the truck.

Contact management first

Ask about vendor registration, insurance requirements, security check-in and permitted service hours. Confirm which entrance and loading area can be used.

Document garage restrictions

Provide floor level, clearance height, ramp width, turns, parked-car spacing and whether the vehicle rolls. Photos of signs and access points can prevent the wrong equipment from being dispatched.

Coordinate keys and authorization

Identify who will meet the driver, open gates and authorize the vehicle transfer. Property access does not replace proof of ownership. Keep copies of completed records.
