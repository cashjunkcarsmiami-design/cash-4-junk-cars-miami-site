Paperwork to Have Available

A vehicle title is commonly used to confirm ownership. A government-issued photo ID, current or previous registration, lien-release documents when applicable, and the vehicle keys may also be helpful. The exact requirements depend on the vehicle and ownership situation.

Do not sign or alter a title until you know how it should be completed. If a name is different, an owner is unavailable, a lien appears on the title, or the title was lost, explain that before scheduling pickup.

  • Vehicle title, if available.
  • Government-issued photo identification.
  • Registration or other available ownership records.
  • Lien release, if a lien is shown but has been paid.
  • Keys, remote controls and any alarm instructions.

Before the Tow Truck Arrives

  1. Remove personal property from the interior and trunk.
  2. Locate the title, ID, keys and any available registration.
  3. Tell us whether the tires roll and whether the vehicle can steer.
  4. Confirm that a driver can safely reach the vehicle.
  5. Ask questions about paperwork before the appointment.

Never leave important documents or personal items inside a vehicle after it is removed. Clear information helps the driver bring the correct equipment and keeps the appointment efficient.
