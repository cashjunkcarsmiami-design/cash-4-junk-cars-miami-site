Arranging a Car Sale From Valet Storage in Miami Beach

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 5, 2026

A vehicle in valet-managed storage may be held away from the entrance where it was dropped off. Before a Miami Beach sale inquiry, confirm the actual location and who can make the car accessible for collection.

Find the actual storage position

Ask the valet or property contact where the car is parked and which entrance is used for release. Provide that location to the buyer rather than only a hotel or building name. Explain hours, clearance information supplied by the property and whether staff must be present. A buyer cannot confirm access to a managed facility without the relevant arrangements.

Clarify keys and current movement

Identify who holds the keys and whether the car starts, rolls and steers. State if its condition changed while stored. If only old photographs are available, disclose that and explain what has been observed recently. Ask the property contact about release procedures or existing charges separately from the buyer's offer; a vehicle quote does not settle those matters.

Match the two appointment arrangements

Coordinate property access with the buyer's collection window and establish who will attend the handoff. Confirm ownership documents and the offer before arranging release. If the car is moved to another facility or access changes, update both contacts. Do not treat a pickup request as a confirmed booking until the vehicle location and release plan are agreed.

Is the drop-off entrance always the pickup location?

No. Confirm the car's actual storage position and the release entrance with the valet or property contact.
