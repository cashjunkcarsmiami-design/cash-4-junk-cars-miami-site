Clear Pickup Directions for a Westchester Car Sale

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 10, 2026

Several similar driveways or cars on one street can make a pickup description ambiguous. After a Westchester car sale is arranged, explain the correct approach and vehicle position clearly. Collection directions should identify the agreed car without relying on a landmark that may be missing when the driver arrives.

Confirm the actual collection address

Use the location where the car is currently parked, not an old residence or billing address. Explain which driveway or entrance provides access and whether there is another vehicle nearby that looks similar. Share the specific collection details with the buyer once arrangements are confirmed, and keep the city and ZIP code consistent with the original inquiry.

Use stable descriptions for the car position

Describe the side of the property, surface and nearby fixed features. A neighbor's parked truck or a temporary sign may not be present later. Send a current view of the approach if requested, avoiding unrelated people or private documents in the photograph. Tell the buyer if the car sits behind another vehicle rather than appearing accessible from the street.

Arrange one contact for arrival questions

Confirm who can answer the driver and provide access during the collection window. If the address or car position changes, call the buyer before arrival rather than sending an unexplained replacement photo. Keep directions separate from vehicle condition updates so the agreed offer, selected car and arrival plan remain easy to follow.

Is the ZIP code enough to book collection?

It helps begin a quote, but collection requires the actual location and access arrangement. Confirm those details with the buyer before treating a pickup time as final.
