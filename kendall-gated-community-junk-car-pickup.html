Junk Car Pickup From a Kendall Gated Community

Entry approval and accurate parking details can prevent delays at the gate.

CallKendall Service

Confirm Community Permission

Ask management or security whether a tow truck needs advance notice, a vendor entry, proof of insurance or a specific arrival window. Follow the property rules and never publish gate codes online.

Describe the Route to the Car

Share the correct entrance, building or house number, parking space and any narrow turns, height restrictions or parked vehicles that affect access.

Prepare the Vehicle

Remove personal items and make the keys available. Tell the buyer if the wheels are locked, tires are flat, steering is damaged or the car cannot shift into neutral.

Coordinate Arrival

Keep your phone available when the driver approaches. A resident may need to meet the truck at the entrance or authorize access with security.

Details to Send Before Dispatch

Provide the community name, approved entrance, building or house number and the location of the vehicle. Describe garage clearance, speed bumps, narrow streets and any visitor parking rules. The buyer also needs the year, make, model, title status and whether the vehicle starts, rolls, steers or has flat tires. Photos of the car and access route help determine whether normal flatbed pickup is appropriate.

Prepare for a Smooth Kendall Pickup

Obtain permission from management before the appointment and keep your phone available when the driver approaches. Remove personal items, prepare the keys and ownership documents and confirm the agreed offer before releasing the vehicle. Never publish a gate code on a public page. Kendall Access Questions

Should I notify the HOA?

Ask the property manager or association which rules apply before confirming pickup.

Why does garage height matter?

A standard tow truck may not fit under every structure, so height and ramp information should be shared early.
