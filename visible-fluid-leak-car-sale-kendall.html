Reporting a Visible Fluid Leak When Selling a Car in Kendall

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 10, 2026

A spot under a parked car is a condition detail worth explaining. For a Kendall junk car offer, report what you can see and what is known from earlier work. You do not need to identify an unknown fluid or run a damaged car to make the description sound more definite.

Separate visible facts from suspected causes

Describe where the spot appears, whether it is recent and whether the car was moved or operated before it appeared. If a mechanic identified a leak, share that explanation with its timing. Otherwise say the fluid or source is unknown. A photograph of the visible area can help the buyer ask follow-up questions without converting a guess into a diagnosis.

Explain how the car currently operates

Say whether it starts, moves or has been left parked because of the problem. Do not start it simply to reproduce a leak for a quote. Include other known symptoms and any repair attempt that changed its condition. The buyer should understand the vehicle in its present state, not an older description from before the leak appeared.

Coordinate collection from its actual position

Describe the surface and access where the car is parked, and mention the visible leak when arranging pickup. Do not attempt fluid disposal or underneath repairs just to prepare for the sale. Ask the buyer to confirm acceptance, the amount and collection arrangements for that condition, then report a significant change before the driver arrives.

Should I repair a leak before asking for an offer?

You can request a review of the car as it is. Explain the visible condition and known history, then ask what information the buyer needs. A repair cost does not guarantee an equivalent increase in the vehicle offer.
