Planning Junk Car Pickup From a Hallandale Beach Apartment Lot

Apartment pickups work best when the resident, management and driver all know the approved entrance, vehicle location and time window.

Verify the parking rules

Ask management whether outside towing vendors require permission, insurance or a resident escort. Confirm the exact deadline when the vehicle is under a property notice.

Make the car easy to identify

Send the building, space number, color, make and model. Explain missing keys, flat tires, locked steering or another vehicle blocking access.

Protect neighbors and property

Choose an approved time, keep the loading path clear and avoid blocking fire lanes or resident traffic. Do not promise same-day removal until ownership, access and equipment have been confirmed.

Confirm That the Property Authorizes Removal

Ask management whether the registered owner must be present and if the tow truck needs a vendor pass, reserved space or specific arrival time. Share the building, parking-space number and approved entrance. Never schedule removal of a vehicle you do not own or have authority to sell.
