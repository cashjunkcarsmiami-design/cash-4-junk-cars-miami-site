Preparing a Junk Car With Missing Keys

Locked steering and wheels can turn a simple pickup into a different towing job.

CallLocal Service

State Exactly What Is Missing

Explain whether every key is lost, a key exists but does not turn, the battery is dead or the doors are locked. Do not promise that the car rolls if that has not been confirmed.

Check Wheel and Steering Position

Tell the buyer if wheels are turned toward a curb, steering is locked, tires are flat or brakes are seized. Photos of every wheel and the space around the car help with planning.

Create a Clear Loading Path

Move other vehicles and obstacles when possible. Share driveway width, surface and whether the car is behind a gate, fence or carport.

Keep Proof of Ownership Ready

Missing keys do not replace ownership requirements. Confirm which title, registration and identification documents must be reviewed.

Missing-Key Questions

Does a missing key change the offer?

It may affect parts, condition or pickup requirements, so disclose it before the quote is finalized.

Should I pay for a replacement key first?

Ask for a vehicle-specific comparison before spending money; the answer depends on the car and situation.
