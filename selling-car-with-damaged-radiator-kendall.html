Requesting an Offer for a Car With Radiator Damage in Kendall

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 5, 2026

A radiator problem may be part of a larger mechanical or collision issue. A Kendall seller should explain what is known about the damage and the car's present condition without presenting an unverified repair estimate as a diagnosis.

Separate confirmed damage from symptoms

Say whether a repair professional identified radiator damage or whether you observed a leak, warning or visible front-end damage. Mention overheating history if known and state when the vehicle was last used. Do not promise that replacing one part will restore normal operation. Tell the buyer which findings come from a report and which remain uncertain.

Provide useful current details

Give the year, model, overall condition and any removed parts. Existing repair paperwork and accessible photos can help an initial review. Explain if an older image predates disassembly or further damage. There is no need to operate the vehicle just to produce proof for a quote; describe its last known behavior and any limitation on assessing it now.

Confirm an as-is pickup arrangement

State whether the vehicle can roll, whether keys and documents are available and where it is parked. Ask for an offer based on the information provided and confirm which checks remain. Make sure the collection plan reflects the current vehicle rather than its condition before the problem. Send an update if repairs or additional damage change that description.

Will a buyer assume the engine is fine if the radiator is damaged?

Do not assume that. Describe the known history and make clear when the engine condition has not been assessed.
