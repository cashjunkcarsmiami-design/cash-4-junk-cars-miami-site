Questions about selling a car in Miami Gardens

Can I get an offer for an accident-damaged car?

Yes. Describe the damage, missing components and whether the wheels or steering are affected. Photos help with the initial review; the offer and collection plan depend on the vehicle and access.

What if the car has no battery or keys?

Tell us before scheduling. Missing keys or a battery can affect access and loading. Do not assume the car can be moved normally; describe its actual position and ask us to confirm the arrangements.
