Changed the Pickup Address After a Junk Car Quote in Miami?

Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · October 29, 2026

Update the buyer before moving a car after a quote. Reconfirm coverage, vehicle condition, access and included pickup at the new Miami-area location.

Treat the new address as a new pickup review

A junk car offer is based on more than the car itself. A vehicle quoted at an accessible driveway may later be at a repair shop, storage lot or another home. Tell Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami about the change before relying on the earlier pickup plan. A confirmed city name does not establish coverage for every address, and a pickup arrangement at the first location does not automatically carry over.

Confirm coverage before paying to move the vehicle

Our usual collection area is approximately 20 miles from our Miami base near 2965 NW 101 St. The base does not accept customer visits or vehicle drop-offs. Call 305-457-0817 with the proposed location before arranging a separate move. Some locations, including Homestead and parts of Cutler Bay, may be outside the usual area; acceptance and towing inclusion need explicit confirmation. Do not move the car to the base address in an attempt to complete the sale.

Describe what is different at the destination

Give the new address and explain the parking surface, entrance, gates and available space. State whether the car still has the same wheels, keys and components and whether it can roll and steer. If the move caused damage or changed the vehicle’s condition, report that separately. A photograph of the car alone does not show a low garage entrance, a locked business lot or another vehicle blocking the exit.

Replace the old instructions with one current message

Identify the car by year, make and model, reference the earlier conversation and state clearly that the old address is no longer the pickup point. Send the revised location privately to the buyer through your agreed contact channel. Ask for confirmation of the purchase amount, included collection, remaining paperwork and new collection window. If coverage is not confirmed, leave the visit unbooked rather than assuming the previous appointment still applies.

A practical message to send

“I asked about my [year, make and model] at [old area]. It is now at [new address]. The condition is [unchanged or explain changes], and access is [describe entrance and restrictions]. Please confirm whether you can collect it here, whether pickup is included and whether the earlier offer needs review.” Use the local service pages below for context, then confirm your exact address by phone.
