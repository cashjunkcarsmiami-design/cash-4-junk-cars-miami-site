Selling an Inherited Junk Car in Kendall: What to Confirm

Possessing an inherited vehicle does not automatically prove authority to sell it. Resolve ownership questions before scheduling pickup or promising a transfer date.

Identify the legal owner

Review the title, registration and estate documents. Names and signatures must match the authority used for the sale. Requirements can vary, so contact the appropriate Florida agency or a qualified professional when the estate or title is unresolved.

Do not guess about missing documents

Tell the buyer exactly which documents exist. Avoid signing another person’s name or presenting incomplete paperwork as final authorization. Ask what can be reviewed before an offer is scheduled.

Prepare vehicle and pickup details

After authority is confirmed, provide the VIN, condition, location, keys and access restrictions. Remove personal property and keep copies of all completed transfer records.
