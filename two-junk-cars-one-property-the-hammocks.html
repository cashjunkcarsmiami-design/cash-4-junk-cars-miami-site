Selling Two Unwanted Cars From One Property in The Hammocks

By Cash 4 Junk Cars Miami · September 21, 2026

Two unwanted cars at the same address do not necessarily share the same value, condition or pickup plan. If you want to sell both from a property in The Hammocks, describe them as separate vehicles from the beginning. Then ask whether their collection can be coordinated.

Create one short description per car

List each vehicle's year, make, model, keys, known faults and available documents. Label photos clearly so a picture of the running car is not attached to the non-running car's description. If different people own the vehicles, explain that before discussing a combined handoff.

Clarify individual and combined amounts

Ask whether a quoted figure refers to one vehicle or both. Keep a clear record of any individual amounts and the conditions attached to each. Do not assume two cars will receive identical offers because they are parked together, or that accepting one automatically means you have accepted the other.

Explain the parking order

Tell the team if one vehicle blocks the other, if they face different directions or if access changes when one is removed. Describe the situation without pushing cars around yourself. The pickup crew should decide whether collection can happen together or needs separate arrangements.

Confirm the scope of the appointment

Before pickup, confirm exactly which vehicles are being purchased and who will be available for each transaction. If you decide to keep one, notify the buyer early so the offer and plan can be reviewed.
